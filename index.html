About

Built on learning, values and leadership

The school aims for strong academics alongside sports, creativity and character.

🏫

Who they are

A higher secondary school in Mysuru, run from kindergarten to pre-university and managed by the J. Ranganna Lakshmamma Charitable Trust.

🎯

What they aim for

Modern skills, ethics and leadership, supported by sports, creative activities and hands-on projects.

📍

Where they are

In the Kanakadasa Nagar and Dattagalli area of Mysuru, a residential part of the city.

Journey

Milestones along the way

2007

The school is established in Mysuru.

2008

Receives CBSE affiliation and follows the national curriculum.

2017

Becomes the first school in Mysuru to open an Atal Tinkering Lab, inaugurated in September 2017.

2018

Wins the British Council International School Award.

2019

Wins the Wipro Earthian Award for sustainability initiatives.

2025

A space lab is reported to be opening at the school in February 2025.
